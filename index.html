About LMHA

The Lebanon Mental Health Association (LMHA) is dedicated to advancing mental health and emotional well-being across Lebanon. We believe that mental health is an essential part of overall health and that every individual deserves access to understanding, support, and appropriate care without stigma or discrimination.

Our work focuses on increasing mental health awareness, encouraging early recognition of mental health challenges, promoting prevention, and helping individuals and families better understand when and how to seek support. Through research, education, advocacy, training, community engagement, and mental health services, LMHA works to strengthen the connection between scientific knowledge and the everyday mental health needs of the community.

We address mental health across different stages of life, recognizing that children, adolescents, adults, older people, families, caregivers, and vulnerable communities may face different psychological and social challenges. Our aim is to contribute to an environment where conversations about mental health are open, informed, and free from stigma, and where seeking professional support is recognized as a positive and important step.

By bringing together mental health professionals, researchers, educators, community members, and partner organizations, LMHA seeks to promote prevention, strengthen mental health literacy, support evidence-based approaches, and contribute to healthier and more resilient communities throughout Lebanon.

Vision

To build a society where mental health is recognized as an essential part of overall well-being and where every individual can seek support without stigma or discrimination.

Mission

To promote mental well-being through awareness, education, research, advocacy and accessible support while empowering individuals and communities with the knowledge and resources needed to improve mental health.
