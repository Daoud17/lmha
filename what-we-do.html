02 Advocacy & Awareness

Championing Mental Health as a Basic Right

At LMHA, we believe that mental health is a fundamental human right. Through our advocacy and awareness initiatives, we work to challenge stigma, influence policies and promote mental wellbeing across all age groups.

1. Public Awareness Campaigns

We organize awareness campaigns to educate the public about mental health issues, reduce stigma and encourage open conversations. These initiatives can use social media, community outreach and traditional media to reach diverse audiences.

2. Policy Advocacy

LMHA works with policymakers, healthcare professionals and organizations to encourage the integration of mental health into health policies and improve access to appropriate mental health services.

3. Community Engagement & Training

We conduct awareness sessions, workshops and training activities for schools, workplaces and community groups to promote mental wellbeing and improve mental health literacy.

4. Media & Educational Resources

Through media and educational content, we aim to provide reliable mental health information and practical guidance that can be understood and accessed by the wider community.

5. Awareness for Vulnerable Groups

Awareness initiatives can be adapted for older adults, refugees, caregivers, frontline workers and other groups facing specific mental health challenges.

Join Us in Raising Awareness

Everyone can contribute to better mental health awareness. Individuals, organizations and decision-makers can support this mission by sharing reliable information, participating in initiatives and building partnerships that promote a healthier society.

03 Training & Education

Building Knowledge and Mental Health Skills

LMHA offers specialized training initiatives designed to strengthen mental health knowledge and practical skills across different sectors.

These programs support workplace wellbeing, empower educators and provide caregivers with essential knowledge and tools.

Mental Health in the Workplace Training

Specialized sessions can help improve mental health awareness within workplaces by addressing topics such as stress management, coping strategies and the creation of supportive working environments.

Training for Educators

Training programs for educators focus on recognizing potential mental health difficulties among students and developing strategies to provide appropriate support and referrals when needed.

Vocational Training for Caregivers of Alzheimer's Patients

Caregiver training addresses the unique challenges involved in supporting people living with Alzheimer's disease, including communication, coping strategies and the wellbeing of both patients and caregivers.

04 Counseling

Comprehensive Mental Health Support

LMHA supports a multidisciplinary approach to mental health care designed to respond to the different needs of children, adults, older adults and families.

Psychiatrists

Psychiatrists assess, diagnose and manage a wide range of mental health conditions through appropriate medical and therapeutic approaches.

Psychologists

Psychologists provide evidence-based psychological support and therapeutic counseling to help individuals understand and manage mental health challenges.

Psychiatric Nurses

Psychiatric nurses provide ongoing care and support while working closely with psychiatrists and other mental health professionals.

Public Health Practitioners

Public health practitioners focus on prevention, community education and wider initiatives that promote mental wellbeing at the population level.

Social Workers

Social workers help individuals and families access community resources, receive psychosocial support and navigate challenging social circumstances.

Case Managers

Case managers provide personalized support to help individuals navigate services, resources and treatment options.

They can assist with coordination of care, access to appropriate resources and understanding the different steps involved in an individual's mental health journey.
